Our Story

Discover the people and purpose behind Pacific Trails Resort.

Welcome to Pacific Trails Resort

Pacific Trails Resort was created for guests who want more than a place to stay. Our resort offers a peaceful coastal retreat where comfort, nature, and adventure come together. Surrounded by scenic trails, ocean views, and relaxing spaces, Pacific Trails gives every guest the chance to rest, recharge, and enjoy the beauty of the outdoors. We believe a great getaway should feel personal, memorable, and refreshing.

Our mission is to provide warm hospitality while helping guests connect with the natural beauty of the Pacific coast. Whether visitors come for hiking, quiet reflection, family vacations, or a weekend escape, our team works together to make every stay welcoming and meaningful. Pacific Trails Resort is built on care, comfort, and a love for the outdoors, and that spirit is reflected in the people who represent our resort every day.

Our Values

Hospitality, relaxation, outdoor adventure, and respect for nature guide everything we do.

Meet the Team

Emily Carter

Founder and Resort Director

Emily Carter founded Pacific Trails Resort with the vision of creating a destination where guests could enjoy both relaxation and outdoor adventure. She leads the resort with a focus on hospitality, comfort, and unforgettable coastal experiences.

Sofia Bennett
